# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

## Additional Project Management Personas

These focused roles can be assigned when a project’s coordination needs warrant them. They support, rather than replace, the existing Project Manager, Product Manager, and Developer roles.

### Dependency Manager

#### Responsibilities
- Maintain a cross-team dependency register with owners and due dates.
- Surface blocked dependencies early and coordinate mitigation plans.

#### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with the Project Manager to track schedule impact and escalate unresolved blockers.
- Partners with Developers to resolve technical dependencies and with Product Managers to clarify sequencing priorities.

### Change Control Lead

#### Responsibilities
- Intake proposed scope changes and confirm the business context.
- Assess impacts to timeline, cost, risk, and quality; record decisions and follow-up actions.

#### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Facilitates approve, defer, or reject decisions with the Project Manager and relevant sponsors.
- Aligns with the Product Manager on prioritization and with Developers on feasibility and delivery impact.

### Delivery Coordinator

#### Responsibilities
- Track readiness criteria for milestones and release handoffs.
- Coordinate checkpoints across product, engineering, and operations, and make unresolved blockers visible in status reporting.

#### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with the Project Manager to align milestones, handoffs, and status updates.
- Confirms acceptance and sequencing with the Product Manager and coordinates technical readiness with Developers.

### Quality & Compliance Liaison

#### Responsibilities
- Define and monitor adherence to project quality and process gates.
- Align project artifacts with applicable standards and controls, and coordinate closure of gaps before release.

#### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partners with Developers on testing evidence and resolution of quality gaps.
- Coordinates with the Project Manager on gate status and risks, and with the Product Manager on acceptance criteria.

### Stakeholder Engagement Lead

#### Responsibilities
- Define stakeholder audiences, communication channels, and cadence.
- Gather feedback, route action items to owners, and set expectations about risks, trade-offs, and timeline changes.

#### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Coordinates messaging and escalation with the Project Manager.
- Aligns stakeholder updates with the Product Manager’s roadmap and gathers delivery information from Developers.

### Expected Project Outcomes

Explicit ownership of dependency tracking, change decisions, delivery handoffs, quality gates, and stakeholder communications reduces role ambiguity and missed handoffs. Earlier visibility into risks and scope impacts supports better governance, faster decisions, and more predictable project outcomes.
