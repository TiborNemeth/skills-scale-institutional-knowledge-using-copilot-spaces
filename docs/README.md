# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This README is the centralized entry point for OctoAcme project management process documentation. It summarizes the project lifecycle and links to the detailed guides below.

## Project Management Process Summary

OctoAcme uses an iterative, customer-focused approach to deliver work in clear, testable increments:

- **Initiation:** Validate the business need, define measurable goals, align stakeholders, and decide whether the work is ready for planning.
- **Planning:** Turn approved work into a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria, estimates, milestones, and identified risks and dependencies.
- **Execution & tracking:** Deliver and review work in small increments, track progress through regular team rhythms and project boards, and escalate blockers.
- **Risk & communication:** Maintain and review a risk register, provide regular stakeholder updates, and follow defined escalation paths.
- **Release & deployment:** Complete quality and readiness checks, deploy and verify the release, and use rollback and incident guidance if needed.
- **Retrospective & continuous improvement:** Reflect after sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents, then track a small set of owned improvement actions.
- **Roles & personas:** Clarify how Developers, Product Managers, and Project Managers contribute to delivery, alongside QA, stakeholders, and product leads.
